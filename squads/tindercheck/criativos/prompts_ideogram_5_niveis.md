# 🎯 Prompts Ideogram: TinderCheck (Estratégia 5 Níveis de Consciência)

Estes prompts foram refinados para o **Ideogram v2**, consolidando estética, conceito e texto de overlay em um único bloco, conforme o padrão de alta conversão do projeto.

---

## 🪜 Nivel 1: Inconsciente (Unaware)
**Psychological Hook:** Intuition and subtle behavior changes.
**Headline:** "Woman's intuition never fails."
> **Prompt:** A cinematic, high-quality photo from the perspective of a person in a dark bedroom at night. A woman lies awake in the background with a suspicious look, while in the foreground, a man is secretly using his phone. Overlaid on the top of the image in clean, bold white typography is the text: "A WOMAN'S INTUITION NEVER FAILS". Below it, a smaller cyan neon subtitle says: "Has something changed in his behavior?". Dark moody lighting, blue ambient tones, investigative documentary style, 4k.

**Ad Copy (Primary Text):** You feel it. A subtle change in his pattern. The phone always faced down, the new passwords, the late-night notifications. They call it gossip, he calls you "crazy," but deep down, you know the truth has a digital footprint. Discover what’s really happening behind the screen before it's too late.
**CTA:** Learn More
