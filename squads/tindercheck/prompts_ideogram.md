# 🎨 Prompts para Ideogram v2: Variações TinderCheck (AG7-IMG3)

Estes prompts foram desenhados para manter o estilo "Dark/Investigativo" do squad, com foco em alta conversão e legibilidade de textos no Ideogram.

---

### 🦄 Prompt 1: O Alerta de Intuição (Variação Dossiê)
**Objetivo**: Gerar curiosidade e identificação com a dor.

> **Prompt**: A high-quality photo from the perspective of a person lying in a dark bedroom at night. A hand is holding a modern smartphone. On the screen, a realistic notification bubble with a cyan glowing border says: "Search Complete: 3 active profiles found". The background is dimly lit with blue and purple ambient tones. The atmosphere is dramatic and investigative. The text on the phone is sharp and clearly legible. Cinematic lighting, 4k resolution.

---

### 🦄 Prompt 2: O Mecanismo Único (Variação Tecnológica)
**Objetivo**: Mostrar o poder da ferramenta e passar autoridade.

> **Prompt**: A futuristic digital interface mockup, dark mode theme with neon cyan and deep blue accents. In the center, a prominent loading bar at 85% with the text "Deep Web Scanning...". Logos of Tinder, Instagram, and WhatsApp are being interconnected by glowing digital lines. Below the bar, a large bold text says: "DIGITAL PROTOCOL ACTIVE". The overall look is professional, like a high-end cybersecurity tool. Sleek typography, flat design, high contrast.

---

### 🦄 Prompt 3: A Prova Irrefutável (Variação Social Proof)
**Objetivo**: Materializar o resultado (o alívio/prova).

> **Prompt**: A top-down view of a digital tablet on a desk. The tablet displays a professional PDF report titled "INFIDELITY DOSSIER". The report shows blurred profile pictures with a bright red stamp over them that says "MATCH FOUND". The design is clean but serious. A glowing cyan light reflects off the edges of the tablet. The text is crisp and authoritative. Dark office background, moody lighting, ultra-realistic.

---

## 💡 Dicas para o Ideogram:
- Use a proporção **4:5** para Feed ou **9:16** para Stories.
- Se o texto não sair perfeito na primeira vez, use a função de **Edit/Remix** focando na correção do texto.
- O estilo **"Dark/Neon"** costuma performar muito bem para esse nicho de investigação.
